# 12.2 Born-Haber循环计算晶格能

## 题目

试由以下数据画出 Born-Haber 循环，并计算氯化钾的晶格能 $(\mathrm{kJ} \cdot \mathrm{mol}^{-1})$ 。

$$
\mathrm {K (s)} \longrightarrow \mathrm {K (g)} \quad \Delta H _ {1} = 8 9. 0 \mathrm {k J} \cdot \mathrm {m o l} ^ {- 1}
$$

$$
\mathrm {C l} _ {2} (\mathrm {g}) \longrightarrow 2 \mathrm {C l} (\mathrm {g}) \quad \Delta H _ {2} = 2 4 3 \mathrm {k J} \cdot \mathrm {m o l} ^ {- 1}
$$

$$
\mathrm {K (g)} \longrightarrow \mathrm {K} ^ {+} (\mathrm {g}) + \mathrm {e} \quad \Delta H _ {3} = 4 1 9 \mathrm {k J} \cdot \mathrm {m o l} ^ {- 1}
$$

$$
\mathrm {C l} (\mathrm {g}) + \mathrm {e} \longrightarrow \mathrm {C l} ^ {-} (\mathrm {g}) \quad \Delta H _ {4} = - 3 4 9 \mathrm {k J} \cdot \mathrm {m o l} ^ {- 1}
$$

$$
\mathrm {K (s)} + \frac {1}{2} \mathrm {C l} _ {2} (\mathrm {g}) \longrightarrow \mathrm {K C l (s)} \quad \Delta H _ {5} = - 4 3 6. 5 \mathrm {k J} \cdot \mathrm {m o l} ^ {- 1}
$$


## 参考答案

> **原书解答（2026-08-25 逐字转录）**

_2(g)} \\ \text {K(g) + Cl(g)} \\ \text {K(g) + Cl(g)} \\ \text {K(s) + Cl(g)} \\ \text {K+ (g) + Cl(g) + e} \\ \text {K+ (g) + Cl(g) + e} \\ \text {K+ (g) + Cl(g) + e} \\ \text {K+ (g) + Cl(g) + e} \\ \text {K+ (g) + Cl(g) + e} \\ \text {K+ (g) + Cl(g) + e} \\ \text {K+ (g) + Cl(g) + e} \\ \text {\Delta H_{3}} \\ \text {\Delta H_{4}} \\ \text {\Delta H_{3}} \\ \text {\Delta H_{2}} \\ \text {\Delta H_{1}} \\ \text {\Delta H_{5}} \\ \text {\Delta H = -U} \\ \text {\Delta H} _ {5} \\ \text {\Delta H} _ {1} \\ \text {\Delta H} _ {5} \\ \text {\Delta H} _ {1} \\ \text {\Delta H} _ {5} \\ \text {\Delta H} _ {1} \\ \text {\Delta H} _ {5} \\ \text {\Delta H} _ {1} \\ \text {\Delta H} _ {5} \\ \text {\Delta H} _ {1} \\ \text {\Delta H} _ {5}. \end{array}
$$

$\Delta H_{1}+\frac{1}{2}\Delta H_{2}+\Delta H_{3}+\Delta H_{4}+\Delta H=\Delta H_{5}$ ，即

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & \Delta H _ {1} + \frac {1}{2} \Delta H _ {2} + \Delta H _ {3} + \Delta H _ {4} + (- U) = \Delta H _ {5} \\ & U = \Delta H _ {1} + \frac {1}{2} \Delta H _ {2} + \Delta H _ {3} + \Delta H _ {4} - \Delta H _ {5} \\ & \quad = \left[ 8 9. 0 + \frac {1}{2} \times 2 4 3 + 4 1 9 + (- 3 4 9) - (- 4 3 6. 5) \right] \mathrm{kJ} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} \\ & \quad = 7 1 7 \mathrm{kJ} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} \end{array}
$$
