## 题目

12.4 根据查得的 $NH_{4}Cl(cr)$ 生成焓、晶格能 $(663\ \mathrm{kJ}\cdot\mathrm{mol}^{-1})$ 以及其他必要数据组成循环，计算气态 $NH_{3}$ 的质子亲和焓。


## 参考答案

> **原书解答（2026-08-25 逐字转录）**

$$
\begin{array}{c c c} \frac {1}{2} \mathrm{N} _ {2} (\mathrm{g}) + \frac {3}{2} \mathrm{H} _ {2} (\mathrm{g}) + \frac {1}{2} \mathrm{H} _ {2} (\mathrm{g}) & + & \frac {1}{2} \mathrm{Cl} _ {2} (\mathrm{g}) \xrightarrow {\Delta H _ {\mathrm{f}} (\mathrm{NH} _ {4} \mathrm{Cl})} \mathrm{NH} _ {4} \mathrm{Cl} (\mathrm{s}) \\ \Biggl \downarrow_ {\Delta H _ {1} = \Delta H _ {\mathrm{f}}} & \Biggl \downarrow_ {\Delta H _ {2} = \frac {1}{2} D} & \Biggl \downarrow_ {\Delta H _ {4} = \frac {1}{2} D} \\ \mathrm{NH} _ {3} (\mathrm{g}) + & \mathrm{H} (\mathrm{g}) & \mathrm{Cl} (\mathrm{g}) \\ & \Biggl \downarrow_ {\Delta H _ {3} = I _ {1}} & \Biggl \downarrow_ {\Delta H _ {5} = - E} \\ & \mathrm{H} ^ {+} (\mathrm{g}) \xrightarrow {\Delta H} \mathrm{NH} _ {4} ^ {+} (\mathrm{g}) & \mathrm{Cl} ^ {-} (\mathrm{g}) \\ & & \Delta H _ {6} = - U \end{array}
$$

$$
\Delta H _ {\mathrm{f}} (\mathrm{NH} _ {4} \mathrm{Cl}) = \Delta H _ {1} + \Delta H _ {2} + \Delta H _ {3} + \Delta H _ {4} + \Delta H _ {5} + \Delta H + \Delta H _ {6}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \Delta H & = \Delta H _ {\mathrm{f}} (\mathrm{NH} _ {4} \mathrm{Cl}) - \Delta H _ {1} - \Delta H _ {2} - \Delta H _ {3} - \Delta H _ {4} - \Delta H _ {5} - \Delta H _ {6} \\ & = (- 3 1 4. 4 + 4 5. 9 - \frac {1}{2} \times 4 3 6 - 1 3. 6 0 \times 9 6. 5 - \frac {1}{2} \times 2 4 3 + 3. 6 1 \times 9 6. 5 + 6 6 3) \mathrm{kJ} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} \\ & = - 9 0 8 \mathrm{kJ} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} \end{array}
$$
