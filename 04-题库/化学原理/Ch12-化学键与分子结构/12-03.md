## 题目

12.3 根据查得的 BaO 生成焓及其他数据, 按 Born-Haber 循环计算氧的第二电子亲和能 (Ba 的 $I_{2}=10.00\ eV$ , BaO 的晶格能为 $3054\ kJ\cdot mol^{-1}$ )。


## 参考答案

> **原书解答（2026-08-25 逐字转录）**

$$
\begin{array}{c c c} \mathrm {Ba(s)} & + & \frac {1}{2} \mathrm {O_ {2} (g)} \xrightarrow {\Delta H _ {\mathrm{f}}} \mathrm{BaO(s)} \\ \Biggl \downarrow \Delta H _ {1} = S & & \\ \mathrm{Ba(g)} & & \mathrm {O(g)} \\ \Biggl \downarrow \Delta H _ {2} = I _ {1} + I _ {2} & & \\ \mathrm {Ba^ {2 + } (g)} & + & \mathrm {O^ {2 - } (g)} \end{array}
$$

$$
\Delta H _ {\mathrm{f}} = \Delta H _ {1} + \Delta H _ {2} + \Delta H _ {3} + \Delta H _ {4} + \Delta H _ {5} = S + I _ {1} + I _ {2} + \frac {1}{2} D - E _ {1} - E _ {2} - U
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & E _ {2} = S + I _ {1} + I _ {2} + \frac {1}{2} D - E _ {1} - U - \Delta H _ {\mathrm{f}} \\ & = (1 7 7. 8 + 5. 2 1 2 \times 9 6. 5 + 1 0. 0 0 \times 9 6. 5 + \frac {1}{2} \times 4 9 5 - 1. 4 6 1 \times 9 6. 5 - 3 0 5 4 + 5 4 8. 0) \mathrm{kJ} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} \\ & = - 7 5 1 \mathrm{kJ} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} \end{array}
$$
