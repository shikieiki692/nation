## 题目

12.13 写出所有第二周期同核双原子分子的分子轨道表示式，其中哪些分子不能稳定存在？哪些分子是顺磁性，哪些是反磁性?


## 参考答案

> **原书解答（2026-08-25 逐字转录）**

解 $\mathrm{Li}_2$ $(\sigma_{1s})^2 (\sigma_{1s}^*)^2 (\sigma_{2s})^2$

$$
\mathrm{Be} _ {2} \quad (\sigma_ {1 s}) ^ {2} (\sigma_ {1 s} ^ {*}) ^ {2} (\sigma_ {2 s}) ^ {2} (\sigma_ {2 s} ^ {*}) ^ {2}\tag{不能存在}
$$

$$
\mathrm{B} _ {2} \quad \left(\sigma_ {1 \mathrm{s}}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {1 \mathrm{s}} ^ {*}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {2 \mathrm{s}}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {2 \mathrm{s}} ^ {*}\right) ^ {2} \left(\pi_ {2 p _ {v}}\right) ^ {1} \left(\pi_ {2 p _ {z}}\right) ^ {1}\tag{稳定，顺磁性}
$$

$$
\mathrm{C} _ {2} \quad \left(\sigma_ {1 s}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {1 s} ^ {*}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {2 s}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {2 s} ^ {*}\right) ^ {2} \left(\pi_ {2 p _ {v}}\right) ^ {2} \left(\pi_ {2 p _ {z}}\right) ^ {2}\tag{稳定，反磁性}
$$

$$
\mathrm{N} _ {2} \quad \left(\sigma_ {1 \mathrm{s}}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {1 \mathrm{s}} ^ {*}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {2 \mathrm{s}}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {2 \mathrm{s}} ^ {*}\right) ^ {2} \left(\pi_ {2 \mathrm{p} _ {\mathrm{v}}}\right) ^ {2} \left(\pi_ {2 \mathrm{p} _ {\mathrm{z}}}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {2 \mathrm{p} _ {\mathrm{x}}}\right) ^ {2}\tag{稳定，反磁性}
$$

$$
\mathrm{O} _ {2} \quad (\sigma_ {1 s}) ^ {2} (\sigma_ {1 s} ^ {*}) ^ {2} (\sigma_ {2 s}) ^ {2} (\sigma_ {2 s} ^ {*}) ^ {2} (\sigma_ {2 p _ {x}}) ^ {2} (\pi_ {2 p _ {y}}) ^ {2} (\pi_ {2 p _ {z}}) (\pi_ {2 p _ {y}} ^ {*}) ^ {1} (\pi_ {2 p _ {z}} ^ {*}) ^ {1}
$$

$$
F _ {2} \quad \left(\sigma_ {1 s}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {1 s} ^ {*}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {2 s}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {2 s} ^ {*}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {2 p _ {x}}\right) ^ {2} \left(\pi_ {2 p _ {y}}\right) ^ {2} \left(\pi_ {2 p _ {z}}\right) ^ {2} \left(\pi_ {2 p _ {v}} ^ {*}\right) ^ {2} \left(\pi_ {2 p _ {z}} ^ {*}\right) ^ {2}
$$

$$
\mathrm{Ne} _ {2} \left(\sigma_ {1 s}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {1 s} ^ {*}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {2 s}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {2 s} ^ {*}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {2 p _ {x}}\right) ^ {2} \left(\pi_ {2 p _ {y}}\right) ^ {2} \left(\pi_ {2 p _ {z}}\right) ^ {2} \left(\pi_ {2 p _ {y}} ^ {*}\right) ^ {2} \left(\pi_ {2 p _ {z}} ^ {*}\right) ^ {2} \left(\sigma_ {2 p _ {x}} ^ {*}\right) ^ {2}
$$
