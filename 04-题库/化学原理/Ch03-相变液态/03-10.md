# 3.10 干燥空气通过盛水气瓶后饱和湿空气体积及分压

## 题目

在 293 K 和 101.3 kPa 条件下, 有 $1.00 \, \mathrm{dm}^{3}$ 干燥空气 (设其体积分数为: $\mathrm{O}_{2}$ 21.0%, $\mathrm{N}_{2}$ 79.0%) 通过盛水气瓶后, 饱和湿空气的总体积应是多少? 湿空气中各气体的分压又是多少?

## 参考答案

$$
V_{\text{总}} = \frac{p_{\text{总}} V(\mathrm{O_{2}, N_{2}})}{p(\mathrm{O_{2}, N_{2}})} = \frac{101.3 \, \mathrm{kPa} \times 1.00 \, \mathrm{dm^{3}}}{101.3 \, \mathrm{kPa} - 2.34 \, \mathrm{kPa}} = 1.02 \, \mathrm{dm^{3}}
$$

湿空气中：$p(\mathrm{H_{2}O}) = 2.34 \, \mathrm{kPa}$

$$
p(\mathrm{O_{2}, N_{2}}) = 101.3 \, \mathrm{kPa} - 2.34 \, \mathrm{kPa} = 99.0 \, \mathrm{kPa}
$$

$$
p(\mathrm{O_{2}}) = p(\mathrm{O_{2}, N_{2}}) \times 21.0\% = 99.0 \, \mathrm{kPa} \times 0.210 = 20.8 \, \mathrm{kPa}
$$

$$
p(\mathrm{N_{2}}) = p(\mathrm{O_{2}, N_{2}}) \times 79.0\% = 99.0 \, \mathrm{kPa} \times 0.790 = 78.2 \, \mathrm{kPa}
$$
