6-2 某有机物 X 的热分解为链反应, 其反应机理可如下表示:

$$
\begin{array}{c} \textsf {X} \xrightarrow {k _ {1}} \textsf {X} \bullet + \textsf {H} \bullet \\ \textsf {X} \bullet \xrightarrow {k _ {2}} \textsf {Y} \bullet + \textsf {Z} \\ \textsf {X} + \textsf {Y} \bullet \xrightarrow {k _ {3}} \textsf {X} \bullet + \textsf {W} \\ \textsf {X} \bullet + \textsf {Y} \bullet \xrightarrow {k _ {4}} \textsf {G} \end{array}
$$

其中, H·为氢自由基, Z, W, G 均为分解产物. 请作合理的假设推导分解反应的速率方程, 并说明其对 X 的反应级数.

$$
r = - \frac {\mathrm{d} [ \mathrm{X} ]}{\mathrm{d} t} = k _ {1} [ \mathrm{X} ] + k _ {3} [ \mathrm{X} ] [ \mathrm{Y} \cdot ]
$$

对 X·和 Y·利用稳态近似, 有

$$
\frac {\mathrm{d} [ \mathrm{X} \cdot ]}{\mathrm{d} t} = k _ {1} [ \mathrm{X} ] - k _ {2} [ \mathrm{X} \cdot ] + k _ {3} [ \mathrm{X} ] [ \mathrm{Y} \cdot ] - k _ {4} [ \mathrm{X} \cdot ] [ \mathrm{Y} \cdot ] = 0\tag{2}
$$

$$
\frac {\mathrm{d} [ \mathrm{Y} \cdot ]}{\mathrm{d} t} = k _ {2} [ \mathrm{X} \cdot ] - k _ {3} [ \mathrm{X} ] [ \mathrm{Y} \cdot ] - k _ {4} [ \mathrm{X} \cdot ] [ \mathrm{Y} \cdot ] = 0\tag{3}
$$

0.5 分

(2)+(3)，有

$$
k _ {1} [ \mathrm{X} ] - 2 k _ {4} [ \mathrm{X} \cdot ] [ \mathrm{Y} \cdot ] = 0, \text {即} [ \mathrm{X} \cdot ] = \frac {k _ {1} [ \mathrm{X} ]}{2 k _ {4} [ \mathrm{Y} \cdot ]}\tag{4}
$$

(4)再代入(3)，有

$\frac{k_1k_2[\mathrm{X}]}{2k_4[\mathrm{Y}\cdot] - k_3[\mathrm{X}][\mathrm{Y}\cdot] - \frac{k_1[\mathrm{X}]}{2}} = 0,$ 即 $[\mathrm{Y}\cdot ]^2 +\frac{k_1[\mathrm{Y}\cdot]}{2k_3} -\frac{k_1k_2}{2k_3k_4} = 0$ 1分观察知[Y·]为定值,且[Y·] $= \frac{-\frac{k_1}{2k_3} + \sqrt{\frac{k_1^2}{4k_3^2} + \frac{2k_1k_2}{k_3k_4}}}{2} = -\frac{k_1}{4k_3} +\sqrt{\frac{k_1^2}{16k_3^2} + \frac{k_1k_2}{2k_3k_4}}$ 1分故 $r = -\frac{\mathrm{d[X]}}{\mathrm{dt}} = k_{1}[X] + k_{3}[X][Y\cdot ] = \left(\frac{3}{4} k_{1} + \sqrt{\frac{k_{1}^{2}}{16} + \frac{k_{1}k_{2}k_{3}}{2k_{4}}}\right)[\mathrm{X}] = k^{\prime}[X]$ 1分对 $\mathbf{X}$ 为一级反应. 0.5分
