## 由 Arrhenius 公式

$$
E _ {o b s} = E _ {2} + \frac {1}{2} E _ {1} - \frac {1}{2} E _ {6} (1 \text {分})
$$

其中

$$
E _ {1} = B E (\mathrm{I} - \mathrm{I}) = 152.7 \mathrm{kJ/mol} (0.5 \text {分})
$$

对反应 2 来说，

$$
\Delta H^{\ominus} (2) = B E \left(\mathrm{H} _ {3} \mathrm{COC} - \mathrm{H}\right) - B E (\mathrm{H} - \mathrm{I}) = 75.7 \mathrm{kJ/mol} (0.5 \text {分})
$$

系吸热反应，所以其逆反应的活化能可以用提示3里的经验规则计算：

$$
E _ {- 2} = 0.055 B E (\mathrm{H} - \mathrm{I}) = 16.4 \mathrm{kJ/mol(0.5分)}
$$

$$
\text { 故 } E _ {2} = \Delta H^{\ominus} (2) + E _ {- 2} = 92.1 \mathrm{kJ/mol(0.5分)}
$$

$$
E _ {6} = 0 (0.5 \text {分})
$$

$$
E _ {o b s} = E _ {2} + \frac {1}{2} E _ {1} - \frac {1}{2} E _ {6} = 84.1 + 0.5 \times 152.7 = \mathbf {168.5 (k J / m o l)} (0.5 \text {分})
