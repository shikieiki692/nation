5-1 碘作催化剂时，氯苯与氯气在二硫化碳溶液中会发生平行反应(均为2级反应). 设在温度和碘的浓度一定时，氯苯与氯气在二硫化碳溶液中的起始浓度均为0.50 mol/L，反应30 min后，有15%的氯苯转变为 $o-C_{6}H_{4}Cl_{2}$ ，有25%的氯苯转变为 $p-C_{6}H_{4}Cl_{2}$ . 请计算两个反应速率常数 $k_{1}$ 和 $k_{2}$ .

该反应开始的生成物浓度为 0.30min 后，邻二氯苯的浓度 $x_{1}$ ，对二氯苯的浓度 $x_{2}$ 则有：

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & x _ {1} = 0.5 \mathrm{mol/L} \times 15 \% = 0.075 \mathrm{mol/L} \\ & x _ {2} = 0.5 \mathrm{mol/L} \times 25 \% = 0.125 \mathrm{mol/L} \\ & x = x _ {1} + x _ {2} = 0.2 \mathrm{mol/L} \end{array}
$$

总反应速率是两个平行反应速率之和，即

$$
\begin{array}{c} {r = \frac {\mathrm{d} x}{\mathrm{d} t} = \frac {\mathrm{d} x _ {1}}{\mathrm{d} t} + \frac {\mathrm{d} x _ {2}}{\mathrm{d} t} = k _ {1} (a - x) ^ {2} + k _ {2} (a - x) ^ {2} = (k _ {1} + k _ {2}) (a - x) ^ {2}} \\ {\int_ {0} ^ {x} \frac {\mathrm{d} x}{(a - x) ^ {2}} = (k _ {1} + k _ {2}) \int_ {0} ^ {t} \mathrm{d} t} \end{array}
$$

积分得

$$
\frac {1}{a - x} - \frac {1}{a} = (k _ {1} + k _ {2}) t
$$

$$
\left(k _ {1} + k _ {2}\right) = \frac {1}{t} \left(\frac {1}{a - x} - \frac {1}{a}\right) = \frac {1}{30} \left(\frac {1}{0.5 - 0.2} - \frac {1}{0.5}\right) = 0.044 \mathrm{L} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} \cdot \min ^ {- 1}
$$

$$
\frac {k _ {1}}{k _ {2}} = \frac {x _ {1}}{x _ {2}} = \frac {0.075}{0.125} = 0.6
$$

解得

$$
\begin{array}{l} k _ {1} = 1.65 \times 10 ^ {- 2} \mathrm{L} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} \cdot \mathrm{min} ^ {- 1} \\ k _ {2} = 2.75 \times 10 ^ {- 2} \mathrm{L} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} \cdot \mathrm{min} ^ {- 1} \end{array}
$$
