6-2-2 推导任意黏度 $\eta$ 下，笼内重组效率 $F_{\mathrm{CP}}$ 与 $\eta$ 的关系式。由

$$
F _ {\mathrm{cP}} = \frac {k _ {\mathrm{rec}}}{k _ {\mathrm{rec}} + k _ {\mathrm{diff}}}
$$

得

$$
\frac {F _ {\mathrm{cP}}}{1 - F _ {\mathrm{cP}}} = \frac {k _ {\mathrm{rec}}}{k _ {\mathrm{diff}}}
$$

又因为

$$
k _ {\mathrm{diff}} = \frac {K}{\eta}
$$

所以

$$
\frac {F _ {\mathrm{cP}}}{1 - F _ {\mathrm{cP}}} = \frac {k _ {\mathrm{rec}} \eta}{K}
$$

参考条件下同理有

$$
\frac {F _ {\mathrm{cP,0}}}{1 - F _ {\mathrm{cP,0}}} = \frac {k _ {\mathrm{rec}} \eta_ {0}}{K}
$$

两式相除:

$$
\frac {F _ {\mathrm{cP}}}{1 - F _ {\mathrm{cP}}} = \frac {\eta}{\eta_ {0}} \cdot \frac {F _ {\mathrm{cP,0}}}{1 - F _ {\mathrm{cP,0}}}
$$

整理得

$$
F _ {\mathrm{cP}} = \frac {F _ {\mathrm{cP,0}} \eta}{(1 - F _ {\mathrm{cP,0}}) \eta_ {0} + F _ {\mathrm{cP,0}} \eta}
$$

若采用

$$
F _ {\mathrm{cP,0}} = 0.21, \eta_ {0} = 1.10 \mathrm{cP}
$$

则

$$
F _ {\mathrm{cP}} = \frac {0.21 \eta}{0.869 + 0.21 \eta} (5 \text {分})
$$
