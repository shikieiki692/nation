$$
c _ {T} = [ \mathrm{S} ] + [ \mathrm{SH} ^ {+} ] = [ \mathrm{S} ] (1 + K [ \mathrm{H} ^ {+} ]),
$$

$$

$$

$$

由物料守恒可知产物浓度为

$$
[ \mathrm{P} ] (t) = c _ {T} (0) - c _ {T} (t) = c _ {T} (0) \left(1 - e ^ {- k _ {\mathrm{obs}} t}\right).
$$

此时 $k_{obs}=3.43\times10^{-3}\ s^{-1}, t=600\ s$ ，所以

$$
[ \mathrm{P} ] = 1.00 \times 10 ^ {- 4} \left(1 - e ^ {- 3.43 \times 10 ^ {- 3} \times 600}\right) = 8.72 \times 10 ^ {- 5} \mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{L} ^ {- 1}.
$$

由 Beer-Lambert 定律，

$$
A = \varepsilon l [ \mathrm{P} ] = (1.20 \times 10 ^ {4}) (1.000) (8.72 \times 10 ^ {- 5}) = 1.05. 
$$

当 $A = 0.800$ 时，

$$

$$

$$

所以

$$
t = \frac {\ln 3}{k _ {\mathrm{obs}}} = \frac {1.099}{3.43 \times 10 ^ {- 3}} = 3.20 \times 10 ^ {2} \mathrm{s}.
