$$
V _ {m, 2} = \frac {8.314 \times 298}{2 \times 100000} + 3.87 \times 10 ^ {- 5} = 0.012427 \mathrm{m} ^ {3} / \mathrm{mol}

体积变化： $\Delta V = V_{m,2} - V_{m,l} = 0.007433\mathrm{m}^3 /\mathrm{mol}$ （4分，代入过程2分）

$$
W = - 2 \times 100000 \times 0.007433 = - 1487 \mathrm{J} = - 1.49 \mathrm{kJ(1分)}

由热力学基本方程推导内能的体积依赖性：

$$
\left(\frac {\partial U}{\partial V}\right) _ {T} = T \left(\frac {\partial p}{\partial T}\right) _ {V} - p
$$

由状态方程得 $p = \frac{RT}{V_{m} - b}$ ，故 $\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_V = \frac{R}{V_{m} - b}$ ，代入得：

$$
\left(\frac {\partial U}{\partial V}\right) _ {T} = \frac {R T}{V _ {m} - b} - p = p - p = 0
$$
