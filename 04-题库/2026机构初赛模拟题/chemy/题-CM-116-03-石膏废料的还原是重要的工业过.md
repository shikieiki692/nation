3-1 反应体系达到平衡， $K_{1}=p^{4}(\mathrm{CO}_{2})/p^{4}(\mathrm{CO})$ ， $K_{2}=p(\mathrm{CO}_{2})p(\mathrm{SO}_{2})/p(\mathrm{CO})p^{\ominus}$ （1分）所以 $K_{2}=K_{1}^{-1/4}\times p(\mathrm{SO}_{2})/p^{\ominus}$ ，代入数据解得 $K_{2}=9.3$ （1分）

$$

**3-2** $3CaSO_{4}(s) + CaS(s) \rightarrow 4CaO(s) + 4SO_{2}(g)$  $K_{3} = 9.7 \times 10^{-6}$ (1分)

$\Delta_{r}G_{m}^{\ominus} = -RT\ln K^{\ominus}, \Delta G_{3} = -8.314 \times 1200 \times ln(9.7 \times 10^{-6}) = 115.17 \text{ kJ} \cdot \text{mol}^{-1}$ (1分)

$\Delta_{r}G_{m}^{\ominus} = \Delta_{r}H_{m}^{\ominus} - T\Delta_{r}S_{m}^{\ominus}, \Delta H_{3} = 1031.2 \text{ kJ} \cdot \text{mol}^{-1}, \Delta S_{3} = 763.4 \text{ J} \cdot \text{mol}^{-1} \cdot \text{K}^{-1}$ (1分)

解得  $S_{m}^{\ominus}(SO_{2}(g)) = 248.2 \text{ J} \cdot \text{mol}^{-1} \cdot \text{K}^{-1}$ (1分)
