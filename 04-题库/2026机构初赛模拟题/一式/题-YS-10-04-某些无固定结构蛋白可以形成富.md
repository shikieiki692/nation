Asp:

$$
\frac {1}{10 ^ {7.36 - 7.00}} \approx 0.304, q _ {\Lambda s p} = 8 \times (- 0.304) = - 2.43
$$

Glu:

$$
f = \frac {1}{1 + 10 ^ {7.98 - 7.00}} \approx 0.095, q _ {\mathrm{Glu}} = 12 \times (- 0.095) = - 1.14 (1 \text {分})
$$

H1 Lys 的凝聚相近似完全质子化:

$$
q _ {\mathrm{Lys}} \approx + 10, q _ {\mathrm{Hls}} = + 5, q _ {\mathrm{Arg}} = + 6 (\mathbf {1} \text {分})
$$

凝聚相总电荷：

$$
Q _ {\mathrm{dense}} \approx - 2.43 - 1.14 + 5 + 10 + 6 = + 17.43 (1 \text {分})
$$
