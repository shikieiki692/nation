本题中 g 取 $10 \, m/s^{2}$ ，已知在 298.15K 下， $\Delta_{\mathrm{f}}\mathrm{H}_{\mathrm{m}}^{\circ}(\mathrm{NO}_{2})=33.18 \, \mathrm{kJ}\cdot\mathrm{mol}^{-1}$ ， $\Delta_{\mathrm{f}}\mathrm{H}_{\mathrm{m}}^{\circ}(\mathrm{N}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{4})=11.10 \, \mathrm{kJ}\cdot\mathrm{mol}^{-1}$ ， $\mathrm{S}_{\mathrm{m}}^{\circ}(\mathrm{NO}_{2})=240.1 \, \mathrm{J}\cdot\mathrm{mol}^{-1}\cdot\mathrm{K}^{-1}$ ， $\mathrm{S}_{\mathrm{m}}^{\circ}(\mathrm{N}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{4})=304.4 \, \mathrm{J}\cdot\mathrm{mol}^{-1}\cdot\mathrm{K}^{-1}$ 。

可能用到的公式:

杠杆力矩平衡： $L_{1}F_{1}=L_{2}F_{2}$

CASIO fx\_991CN CW 迭代（解 $f(x)=0$ ）： $\left(A-\frac{f(x)}{\frac{d}{dx}f(x)\big|_{x=A}}\right)\rightarrow A$

9-1 计算二氧化氮的二聚反应的标准平衡常数。

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \Delta_ {r} H _ {m} ^ {\circ} & = \Delta_ {f} H _ {m} ^ {\circ} (N _ {2} O _ {4}) - 2 \Delta_ {f} H _ {m} ^ {\circ} (N O _ {2}) = 11.10 \mathrm{kJ} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} - 2 \times 33.18 \mathrm{kJ} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} \\ & = - 55.26 \mathrm{kJ} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} \end{array}
$$

$$
\varDelta_ {r} S _ {m} ^ {\circ} = S _ {m} ^ {\circ} (N _ {2} O _ {4}) - 2 S _ {m} ^ {\circ} (N O _ {2}) = 304.4 \mathrm{J} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} \cdot \mathrm{K} ^ {- 1} - 2 \times 240.1 \mathrm{J} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} \cdot \mathrm{K} ^ {- 1}
$$

$$
K ^ {\circ} = \exp \left(- \frac {\varDelta_ {r} G _ {m} ^ {\circ}}{R T}\right) = \exp \left(- \frac {\varDelta_ {r} H _ {m} ^ {\circ} - T \varDelta_ {r} S _ {m} ^ {\circ}}{R T}\right)
$$

$$
= \exp \left(- \frac {- 55.26 \times 10 ^ {3} \mathrm{J} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} - (298.15 \mathrm{K} \times - 175.8 \mathrm{J} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} \cdot \mathrm{K} ^ {- 1})}{8.314 \mathrm{J} \cdot \mathrm{mol} ^ {- 1} \cdot \mathrm{K} ^ {- 1} \times 298.15 \mathrm{K}}\right) = 3.151
$$

9-2 分别计算平衡时 A、B 中的气体组成（以摩尔量表示）。


**9-2** 注意到平衡时杠杆应为A低B高,记支点高度为H,不妨设平衡时A处活塞距支点垂直距离为h,则B处活塞距支点垂直距离为2h。易知A中0.114molN2O4等效于0.228molNO2,不妨令A、B活塞内NO2的初始摩尔量分别为A、B,设二聚反应分别进行了a、b。对K°进行变形: $K^{\circ}=\frac{p(N_{2}O_{4})/p^{\circ}}{(p(NO_{2})/p^{\circ})^{2}}=\frac{p^{\circ}}{p_{\text{总}}}\times\frac{x(N_{2}O_{4})}{x(NO_{2})^{2}}=\frac{p^{\circ}V}{n_{\text{总}}RT}\times\frac{x(N_{2}O_{4})}{x(NO_{2})^{2}}=\frac{p^{\circ}V}{RT}\times\frac{n(N_{2}O_{4})}{n(NO_{2})^{2}}$ 而对于体积V:


$$
V _ {A} = S h _ {A \text {总}} = S (H - h)
$$

$$
V _ {B} = S h _ {B \mathrm{总}} = S (H + 2 h)
$$

对于压力:

$$
F _ {A} = S \left(p _ {A \text {总}} - p _ {a t m}\right) = S \left(\frac {n _ {A \text {总}} R T}{V _ {A}} - p _ {a t m}\right)
$$

$$
F _ {B} = S \left(p _ {B \text {总}} - p _ {a t m}\right) = S \left(\frac {n _ {B \text {总}} R T}{V _ {A}} - p _ {a t m}\right) = \frac {R T (B + b)}{H + 2 h} - p _ {a t m} S
$$

故体系平衡时存在三个约束方程:

$$
\frac {p ^ {\circ} S (H - h)}{R T} \times \frac {a}{(A - 2 a) ^ {2}} = K ^ {\circ}
$$

$$
\frac {p ^ {\circ} S (H + 2 h)}{R T} \times \frac {b}{(B - 2 b) ^ {2}} = K ^ {\circ}
$$

$$
\left(\frac {R T (A - a)}{H - h} - p _ {a t m} S = 2 \times \left(\frac {R T (B - b)}{H + 2 h} - p _ {a t m} S\right) \right.
$$

将 $p^{\circ}=100\ kPa,\quad S=\pi r^{2}=0.785\ dm^{2},\quad T=298.15\ K,\quad H=3\ dm,\quad A=0.228\ mol,\quad B=0.514\ mol,$ $K^{\circ}=3.15,\quad p_{atm}=101.325\ kPa$ 代入，最终解得：

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} h = 1.29 d m \\ a = 0.0939 m o l \\ b = 0.204 m o l \end{array} \right.
$$

故平衡时有:

$$
n (N _ {2} O _ {4}, A) = 0.0939 \mathrm{mol}
$$

$$
n (N O _ {2}, A) = 0.0401 \mathrm{mol}
$$

$$
n (N _ {2} O _ {4}, B) = 0.204 \mathrm{mol}
$$

$$
n (N O _ {2}, B) = 0.106 \mathrm{mol}
$$

9-3

显而易见，应在 A 活塞中通入 $NO_{2}$ ，故设等效后 A 中有 x mol $NO_{2}$ 。平衡时若杠杆水平，则 h=0。
存在约束方程：

$$
\frac {p ^ {\circ} S (H - h)}{R T} \times \frac {a}{(x - 2 a) ^ {2}} = K ^ {\circ}
$$

$$
\frac {p ^ {\circ} S (H + 2 h)}{R T} \times \frac {b}{(B - 2 b) ^ {2}} = K ^ {\circ}
$$

$$
\left(\frac {R T (x - a)}{H - h} - p _ {a t m} S = 2 \times \left(\frac {R T (B - b)}{H + 2 h} - p _ {a t m} S\right) \right.
$$

将 $p^{\circ}=100\ kPa,\ S=\pi r^{2}=0.785\ dm^{2},\ T=298.15\ K,\ H=3\ dm,\ A=0.228\ mol,\ B=0.514\ mol,\ K^{\circ}=3.15,$ $p_{atm}=101.325\ kPa,\ h=0$ 代入，解得：x=0.889 mol。

故应在 A 侧通入 $0.771\ mol\ NO_{2}$ 。

9-4 体系平衡后，欲在某侧活塞上放置一定量的沙子，使得轻杆处于水平状态。指明应在哪一侧放置沙子并计算所需沙子的质量。


**9-4** 显而易见,应在B活塞上放置一定量的沙子,故设B上放置的沙子质量为m。平衡时若杠杆水平,则h=0。存在约束方程: $\left\{\begin{array}{l}\frac{p^{\circ}S(H-h)}{RT}\times\frac{a}{(A-2a)^{2}}=K^{\circ}\\ \frac{p^{\circ}S(H+2h)}{RT}\times\frac{b}{(B-2b)^{2}}=K^{\circ}\\ \frac{RT(A-a)}{H-h}-p_{atm}S=2\times\left(\frac{RT(B-b)}{H+2h}-p_{atm}S-mg\right)\end{array}\right.$ 将 $p^{\circ}=100\mathrm{kPa}, S=\pi r^{2}=0.785\mathrm{dm}^{2}, T=298.15\mathrm{K}, H=3\mathrm{dm}, A=0.228\mathrm{mol}, B=0.514\mathrm{mol}, K^{\circ}=3.15, p_{atm}=101.325\mathrm{kPa}, h=0$ 代入,解得:m=14.8kg。故应在B侧活塞上放置14.8kg的沙子。

**9-5** 若无摩擦,则没有能量的损耗,整体能量守恒,活塞将持续震荡,难以达到静态平衡。
