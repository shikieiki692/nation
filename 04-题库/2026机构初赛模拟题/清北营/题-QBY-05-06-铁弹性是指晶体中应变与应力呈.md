## 6-1 计算其分子量:

$$
\mathbf {M} = \frac {\rho \mathrm{N} _ {\mathrm{A}} \mathrm{V}}{\mathrm{Z}} = \frac {10.67 \times 15.12 \times 10.84 \times \sin 90.49 ^ {\circ} \times 6.022 \times 10 ^ {25} \times 10 ^ {- 30} \times 3.07 ^ {\circ}}{2} = 1621.2 \mathrm{g/mol}
$$
