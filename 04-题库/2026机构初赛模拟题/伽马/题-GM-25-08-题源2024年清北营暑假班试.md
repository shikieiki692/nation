8-1-1 $Na_{3}As$ 的晶胞参数：a = b = 487.4 pm, c = 851.5 pm。计算 $Na_{3}As$ 的密度。由题意可得 Z = 2

$$
\mathrm{D} = \mathrm{ZM/NAV} = \mathrm{ZM/NAa} ^ {2} \mathrm{c}
$$

$$
= 2 \times (22.99 \times 3 + 74.92) / (6.022 \times 10 ^ {23} \times 487.42 \times 851.5 \times 10 ^ {- 30}) = 2.728 (\mathrm{g} / \mathrm{cm} _ {3})
$$
