![](images/d497d16bf0172ba421d1cf46f7744c91d311ad5783d72cae6c9341448510a9f5.jpg)

![](images/3f45d8cc4312fa96e419817049eea690d37869e85f0c09b8418f4ca9793a6543.jpg)

![](images/0bd9bf0bd3a048afe53064d20a41bace8c18761b6a5869bf4525273b394b5f03.jpg)

9-4早期研究该反应时， 研究人员研究了如下底物的反应：

![](images/d3c22fb86ea961bbf89b26a78539216a979bf531563c8dff338f573287b95cea.jpg)
