## 习24 酸碱平衡-盐类水解

0.10 mol/L NaCN溶液的pH为（$K_a(\text{HCN}) = 6.2 \times 10^{-10}$）：

A. 5.1
B. 8.9
C. 11.1
D. 12.0

---

**答案：C**

**解析：**

CN⁻水解：

$$K_b(\text{CN}^-) = \frac{K_w}{K_a} = \frac{1.0 \times 10^{-14}}{6.2 \times 10^{-10}} = 1.61 \times 10^{-5}$$

$$[\text{OH}^-] = \sqrt{K_b \cdot c} = \sqrt{1.61 \times 10^{-5} \times 0.10} = 1.27 \times 10^{-3}$$

$$\text{pOH} = 2.90 \implies \text{pH} = 11.10$$
