## 习22 酸碱平衡-缓冲溶液pH

将0.10 mol HAc和0.050 mol NaOH溶于水配成1.0 L溶液（$K_a(\text{HAc}) = 1.75 \times 10^{-5}$），该缓冲溶液的pH为：

A. 4.14
B. 4.74
C. 4.76
D. 5.34

---

**答案：C**

**解析：**

HAc + NaOH → NaAc + H₂O，NaOH为限制试剂，反应后：

$$[\text{HAc}] = 0.10 - 0.050 = 0.050 \text{ mol/L}$$

$$[\text{Ac}^-] = 0.050 \text{ mol/L}$$

$$\text{pH} = \text{p}K_a + \lg\frac{[\text{Ac}^-]}{[\text{HAc}]} = 4.76 + \lg\frac{0.050}{0.050} = 4.76$$
