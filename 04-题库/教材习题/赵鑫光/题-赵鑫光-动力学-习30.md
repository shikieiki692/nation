## 习30 溶度积-沉淀转化

向AgCl沉淀中加入NaI溶液，可能发生沉淀转化 $\text{AgCl(s)} + \text{I}^- \rightarrow \text{AgI(s)} + \text{Cl}^-$。该反应的平衡常数为：

A. $K_{sp}(\text{AgCl})/K_{sp}(\text{AgI})$
B. $K_{sp}(\text{AgI})/K_{sp}(\text{AgCl})$
C. $K_{sp}(\text{AgCl}) \times K_{sp}(\text{AgI})$
D. $\sqrt{K_{sp}(\text{AgCl})/K_{sp}(\text{AgI})}$

---

**答案：A**

**解析：**

$$K = \frac{[\text{Cl}^-]}{[\text{I}^-]} = \frac{[\text{Cl}^-][\text{Ag}^+]}{[\text{I}^-][\text{Ag}^+]} = \frac{K_{sp}(\text{AgCl})}{K_{sp}(\text{AgI})} = \frac{1.77 \times 10^{-10}}{8.5 \times 10^{-17}} = 2.08 \times 10^{6}$$

$K$ 很大，转化趋于完全，AgCl容易转化为更难溶的AgI。
