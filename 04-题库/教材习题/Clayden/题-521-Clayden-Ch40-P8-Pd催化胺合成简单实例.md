# 题-521: Pd催化胺合成简单实例

## 题目

A synthesis of an antifungal drug made use of this palladium-catalysed reaction. Give a mechanism, explaining the regio- and stereochemistry.

![[06-外部资料导入/clayden 有机习题/课后习题答案及解析clayden-SolutionsManualtoAccompanyOrganicChemistry_400-521_images/db075a72509e15baa7bee208423d1be95877b8feba8dd875bdb344484037a67c.jpg]]

**原文题目**：A synthesis of an antifungal drug made use of this palladium-catalysed reaction. Give a mechanism, explaining the regio- and stereochemistry.

## 参考答案

**Answer (English)**: The palladium forms the usual allyl cation complex and the nitrogen nucleophile attacks the less hindered end thus also retaining the conjugation. Attack at the triple bond would give an allene. The E stereochemistry of the palladium complex is retained in the product.

![[06-外部资料导入/clayden 有机习题/课后习题答案及解析clayden-SolutionsManualtoAccompanyOrganicChemistry_400-521_images/d0c44479b5a8141badafc0f82fd6b8b704a4dc8562b717b8c8c0f27fa408efc5.jpg]]

**中文解析**：

关键步骤：
1. **η³-烯丙基-Pd配合物形成**：Pd(0)氧化加成到烯丙基乙酸酯→η³-烯丙基-Pd(II)配合物
2. **区域选择性**：氮亲核试剂进攻位阻较小的一端→保留共轭体系
3. **为什么不进攻三键？**：进攻三键会生成丙二烯（allene），热力学不利
4. **立体化学**：Pd配合物的E构型→retention→产物E构型保持

> **核心要点**：Pd催化C-N键形成（Buchwald-Hartwig胺化类型）中，亲核试剂的区域选择性由位阻和共轭稳定共同控制。

## 解题思路

1. **读题定位**：Pd催化胺合成→需解释机理、区域和立体化学
2. **关键转换**：Pd(0)→η³-烯丙基→N进攻位阻小端→retention→E产物
3. **验证**：检查区域选择性（非allene）和E构型保持
