# 题-331: 机理复习+烯醇参与的反应

## 题目

Propose mechanisms for the following two reactions involving enol intermediates:

1. Acid-catalyzed hydrolysis of an acetal → non-conjugated enone → isomerization to conjugated enone via enol
2. Nitrile + Grignard reagent → ketone → enolization → bromination at the enol carbon

![[06-外部资料导入/clayden 有机习题/课后习题答案及解析clayden-SolutionsManualtoAccompanyOrganicChemistry_200-399_images/786b54257f302dd723b3c6f428f369235e8c5c4d458d153967a5609ec05111fb.jpg]]

**原文题目**：

为以下两个涉及烯醇中间体的反应提出机理：

1. 缩醛的酸催化水解 → 非共轭烯酮 → 经烯醇异构化为共轭烯酮
2. 腈 + Grignard 试剂 → 酮 → 烯醇化 → 烯醇碳上溴化

## 参考答案

**Answer (English)**:

![[06-外部资料导入/clayden 有机习题/课后习题答案及解析clayden-SolutionsManualtoAccompanyOrganicChemistry_200-399_images/e685c363539522bfa5d7b00aa8e7006c9456cbb174ce75c4ab60750ba2453307.jpg]]

**Reaction 1 — Acetal hydrolysis and enone isomerization**:

Step 1: Acetal hydrolysis. H⁺ protonates one OR group → water displaces ROH → hemiacetal → protonation of second OR → water displaces ROH → ketone/aldehyde. This is the standard acid-catalyzed acetal hydrolysis.

Step 2: The initial product is a **non-conjugated enone** (C=C is not conjugated with C=O). Under acidic conditions, protonation of the C=C double bond at the β-carbon generates a carbocation that is resonance-stabilized by the adjacent C=O → this is equivalent to the **enol** form. Tautomerization of the enol back to the keto form can give the **conjugated enone** (C=C now conjugated with C=O), which is thermodynamically more stable.

**Reaction 2 — Nitrile + Grignard → ketone → enolization → bromination**:

![[06-外部资料导入/clayden 有机习题/课后习题答案及解析clayden-SolutionsManualtoAccompanyOrganicChemistry_200-399_images/1a2c63cdc1bb41ffd108d3d9655cc460c90a366f3e37eefeb73830882850b5b4.jpg]]

Step 1: R-MgBr adds to the nitrile (R-C≡N) to give an imine salt (R-C(=NMgBr)-R'). Acidic workup hydrolyzes the imine to a ketone.

Step 2: Under acidic or basic conditions, the ketone undergoes **enolization** — acid catalyzes protonation of C=O followed by deprotonation at α-carbon to give the enol; or base deprotonates α-H directly to give the enolate.

Step 3: The enol/enolate reacts with Br₂. The α-carbon (enol carbon) is nucleophilic (high HOMO) and attacks Br₂ → C-Br bond formation + loss of H⁺ → α-bromo ketone.

**中文解析**：

**反应 1（缩醛水解→烯酮异构化）**：

步骤 1：标准酸催化缩醛水解——H⁺ 质子化一个 OR → 水取代 ROH → 半缩醛 → 质子化第二个 OR → 水取代 → 醛/酮。

步骤 2：初始产物是**非共轭烯酮**（C=C 未与 C=O 共轭）。酸性条件下，C=C 双键被质子化生成碳阳离子，其被相邻 C=O 共振稳定 → 等价于烯醇中间体。烯醇回到酮式时，C=C 可变为与 C=O 共轭 → 得到热力学更稳定的**共轭烯酮**。

**反应 2（腈+Grignard→酮→α-溴化）**：

步骤 1：R-MgBr 对腈 (R-C≡N) 加成得亚胺盐 → 酸性水解 → 酮。

步骤 2：酸或碱催化下酮**烯醇化**——酸催化：C=O 质子化 → α-H 脱去 → 烯醇；碱催化：α-H 直接脱去 → 烯醇盐。

步骤 3：烯醇/烯醇盐的 α-碳具有亲核性（HOMO 能量高），与 Br₂ 反应 → α-溴代酮。烯醇碳是亲核的，Br₂ 是亲电的 → C-Br 键形成。

## 解题思路

1. **读题定位**：两个机理都涉及烯醇作为关键中间体——第一个是异构化，第二个是α-卤代
2. **🔑 关键转换**：反应1：缩醛水解→非共轭烯酮→质子化→烯醇→共轭烯酮（热力学产物）；反应2：Grignard+腈→酮→烯醇化→亲核α-碳进攻Br₂
3. **验证**：两个反应中烯醇的角色不同——反应1中是异构化中间体，反应2中是亲核试剂

## 易错分析

| 错误 | 原因 | 纠正 | 课堂提问 |

| 反应1中跳过烯醇直接写异构化 | 不理解酸催化异构化的机理 | 必须经过烯醇：质子化C=C→碳阳离子→脱质子→烯醇→酮式 | 为什么共轭烯酮比非共轭烯酮更稳定？ |
