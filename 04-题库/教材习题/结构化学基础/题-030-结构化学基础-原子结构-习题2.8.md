# 题-030：原子结构 习题2.8

> **来源**：周公度 段连运《结构化学基础（第5版）习题解析》第2章
> **难度**：⭐⭐⭐⭐
> **教学层级**：竞赛拔高

---

## 题目

已知氢原子的归一化基态波函数为

$$
\psi_ {1 \mathrm{s}} = (\pi a _ {0} ^ {3}) ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} \exp \left[ - \frac {r}{a _ {0}} \right]
$$

(1) 利用量子力学基本假设求该基态的能量和角动量；

(2) 利用位力定理(即维里定理)求该基态的平均势能和零点能。

---

## 参考答案

解 (1) 根据量子力学关于"本征函数、本征值和本征方程"的假设, 当用 Hamilton 算符作用于 $\psi_{1s}$ 时, 若所得结果等于一常数乘以 $\psi_{1s}$ , 则该常数即氢原子的基态能量 $E_{1s}$ 。氢原子的 Hamilton 算符为

$$
\hat {H} = - \frac {h ^ {2}}{8 \pi^ {2} m} \nabla^ {2} - \frac {e ^ {2}}{4 \pi \varepsilon_ {0} r}
$$

由于 $\psi_{1s}$ 的角度部分是常数，因而 $\hat{H}$ 与 $\theta, \phi$ 无关：

$$
\hat {H} = - \frac {h ^ {2}}{8 \pi^ {2} m} \frac {1}{r ^ {2}} \frac {\partial}{\partial r} \left(r ^ {2} \frac {\partial}{\partial r}\right) - \frac {e ^ {2}}{4 \pi \varepsilon_ {0} r}
$$

将 $\hat{H}$ 作用于 $\psi_{1\mathrm{s}}$ ，有

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \hat {H} \psi_ {1 s} & = \left[ - \frac {h ^ {2}}{8 \pi^ {2} m} \frac {1}{r ^ {2}} \frac {\partial}{\partial r} \left(r ^ {2} \frac {\partial}{\partial r}\right) - \frac {e ^ {2}}{4 \pi \varepsilon_ {0} r} \right] \psi_ {1 s} \\ & = - \frac {h ^ {2}}{8 \pi^ {2} m} \frac {1}{r ^ {2}} \left(2 r \frac {\partial}{\partial r} \psi_ {1 s} + r ^ {2} \frac {\partial^ {2}}{\partial r ^ {2}} \psi_ {1 s}\right) - \frac {e ^ {2}}{4 \pi \varepsilon_ {0} r} \psi_ {1 s} \\ & = \left[ \frac {h ^ {2}}{8 \pi^ {2} m a _ {0} ^ {2}} - \frac {e ^ {2}}{4 \pi \varepsilon_ {0} a _ {0}} \right] \psi_ {1 s} (r = a _ {0}) \end{array}
$$

所以

$$
\begin{array}{r l} E _ {1} & = \frac {h ^ {2}}{8 \pi^ {2} m a _ {0} ^ {2}} - \frac {e ^ {2}}{4 \pi \varepsilon_ {0} a _ {0}} \\ & = 2. 1 8 4 \times 1 0 ^ {- 1 8} \mathrm{J} - 4. 3 6 3 \times 1 0 ^ {- 1 8} \mathrm{J} \\ & = - 2. 1 7 9 \times 1 0 ^ {- 1 8} \mathrm{J} \end{array}
$$

将角动量平方算符作用于氢原子的 $\psi_{1s}$ ，有

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \hat {M} ^ {2} \psi_ {\mathrm{1s}} & = - \left(\frac {h}{2 \pi}\right) ^ {2} \left[ \frac {1}{\sin \theta} \frac {\partial}{\partial \theta} \left(\sin \theta \frac {\partial}{\partial \theta}\right) + \frac {1}{\sin^ {2} \theta} \frac {\partial^ {2}}{\partial \phi^ {2}} \right] (\pi a _ {0} ^ {3}) ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {r}{a _ {0}}} \\ & = 0 \psi_ {\mathrm{1s}} \end{array}
$$

所以

$$
M ^ {2} = 0, \quad | M | = 0
$$

(2) 对氢原子, $V\propto r^{-1}$ ,故

$$
\langle T \rangle = - \frac {1}{2} \langle V \rangle
$$

$$
E _ {1 \mathrm{s}} = \langle T \rangle + \langle V \rangle = \frac {1}{2} \langle V \rangle
$$

$$
\langle V \rangle = 2 E _ {1 s} = 2 \times (- 1 3. 6 \mathrm{eV}) = - 2 7. 2 \mathrm{eV}
$$

$$
\langle T \rangle = - \frac {1}{2} \langle V \rangle = - \frac {1}{2} \times (- 2 7. 2 \mathrm{eV}) = 1 3. 6 \mathrm{eV}
$$

此即氢原子的零点能。
