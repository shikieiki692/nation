$$
\frac {0 . 8 0 \times 1 0 ^ {- 2} \times 4 . 1 \times 1 0 ^ {- 5}}{1 . 4 \times 1 0 ^ {- 3}} = 2. 3 \times 1 0 ^ {- 4}\tag{1 分}

$$
