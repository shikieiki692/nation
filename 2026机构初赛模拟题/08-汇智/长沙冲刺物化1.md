$$
\begin{array}{l l} \Delta T _ {f} = i K _ {f} m, & \Delta T _ {b} = i K _ {b} m, \\ \Pi = i c R T, & p _ {A} = i x _ {A} p _ {A} ^ {0}. \end{array}

$$
